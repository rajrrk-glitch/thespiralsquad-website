The problem

The morning check doesn't scale

At five accounts, checking Meta Ads every morning is tedious. At twenty, it's where things slip: a campaign stops delivering, a paused campaign is still "Live" in the plan, a test ad set quietly spends for a week. AI agents could do the check in seconds, but no agency owner wants an agent that can edit budgets on a hunch.

The idea

Separate seeing from doing

Analyst

Sees everything, changes nothing

Read-only permissions checked at the token level, not just in the prompt. The Analyst cannot write even if asked to.

Orchestrator

The approval gate

Every change is a written brief that a named person approves. Nothing moves without a decision on record.

Executor

Acts only on approved briefs

Logs before/after values. Never deletes. Optional — the Analyst and Orchestrator alone deliver the daily check.
