Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026  ·  The Spiral Squad

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how The Spiral Squad ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our services and our authorised integration with the Meta Marketing API. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Our Meta Marketing API Integration

The Spiral Squad operates an authorised Meta Developer application that connects to the Meta Marketing API. Through this integration, and only where you have expressly authorised us to do so, we may:

  • Retrieve advertising account information associated with your Meta Business account
  • Retrieve campaigns, ad sets, and ads within those accounts
  • Retrieve advertising performance data and insights (such as reach, impressions, clicks, conversions, and spend)
  • Manage advertising assets — including creating or modifying campaigns, ad sets, or ads — where you have explicitly authorised us to do so

Purpose of Data Use

We access your advertising data solely for the following purposes:

  • Advertising management on your behalf
  • Performance reporting and analysis
  • Optimisation of your advertising campaigns
  • Authorised automation of advertising workflows

We do not use your advertising data for any purpose beyond what you have authorised, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own marketing or advertising purposes.

Access Credentials and Tokens

Meta access credentials, including access tokens and API keys, are treated as strictly confidential. We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect these credentials and ensure they are never publicly exposed, shared without authorisation, or used beyond the scope of your explicit permission.

Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. When our engagement with you ends, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify data that is no longer needed.
