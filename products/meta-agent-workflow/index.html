The operational reality

Doing the work isn't the same as checking the work.

Launching a campaign is only one part of managing it.

The real operational problem begins after launch.

  • Something can change.
  • Something can stop spending.
  • Something can overspend.
  • Something can remain live when it shouldn't.
  • Something can simply stop matching the original plan.

The Workflow creates a repeatable checking process around the AI's work.

The goal isn't to pretend AI never makes mistakes. The goal is to make it easier to notice when reality and intention stop matching.

This is about reducing uncertainty — not promising perfection.

Consistency

AI needs context.

The workflow uses a defined source of truth for campaign requirements. A consistent reference for what should be happening.

Instead of repeatedly explaining the campaign to AI every session, the process gives it something to work from. The same brief, every time.

When AI knows what was intended, it can check what actually happened.

That's the foundation of anything reliable.

The separation

Let AI work.
Don't let it work without a process.

The workflow separates three things:

  • 01
    What needs to be done
    The work AI can handle — checking, reporting, surfacing issues. Defined upfront, not improvised session to session.
  • 02
    What needs to be checked
    AI's output isn't trusted blindly. The process builds in verification — so you know what was actually checked, not just what was run.
  • 03
    When something needs you
    Not every issue needs intervention. The workflow surfaces what matters and tells you when a human call is actually required.

The purpose isn't full autonomy. It's controlled execution — with you still in the loop.
