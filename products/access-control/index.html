FAQ

Questions I get asked before people buy.

  • Do I need to know how to code?
    No. Every step has a screenshot. There's no code to write — you're clicking through a dashboard, not writing scripts. If you can follow a Google Doc, you can do this setup.
  • Is this safe for my ad account?
    Yes. It uses Meta's own API and System User infrastructure — not browser automation. You're not mimicking a human clicking around. The access is limited to what you explicitly allow, and Meta built this path for developers and agencies to use.
  • Why not just use Meta's official connector?
    Meta's official connector (and most MCP implementations) work through your login. Whatever your Facebook account can reach, the AI can reach — there's no way to limit it to one client or one ad account. This Blueprint gives you per-account, per-permission scoping that the official connector doesn't support.
  • Is ₹299 just a repackaged YouTube tutorial?
    No. The System User + Developer App path is not well-documented, and most tutorials don't cover it at all. What's in here is: exact screenshots for every step, a checklist, copy-paste test prompts, a troubleshooting section, and ongoing updates. I built it from scratch after weeks of testing.
  • Where do I store the access token after setup?
    The guide tells you where the token goes in your configuration and how to handle it safely. It doesn't prescribe a specific storage tool — that depends on your setup — but it covers the patterns and what to avoid.
  • What do I do after the access is set up?
    The access setup is the foundation. Once it's done, you can connect any MCP-compatible agent (Claude, ChatGPT, and others) to your Meta Ads. If you want the full operating process — how to actually run ad-ops with AI once the access is set up — that's what The Workflow covers.
